Borla

Waste, sorted — one ring away.

A real-time matchmaker that connects households with the roaming tricycle collectors already in their neighbourhood. The design digitizes a familiar act — the collector's bell — into a signal you send and a signal you answer. Built for bright sun, cheap Android phones, and mixed literacy: high contrast, icon-first, one clear action per screen.
